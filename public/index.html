How opting in works

1. Publish your terms

A lab shares its standard employment, exit and equity terms (redacted is fine) plus its speak-up policy.

2. Get scored

Each clause gets a rating, with the exact wording that earned it.

3. Shown here

Opted-in labs are marked. Everyone else stays rated on public evidence only.

Made by Jonathan Hind. Ratings use public evidence only, and "not public" means it can't be checked, not that it's bad. Score = clauses met, plus half for each partly met, out of seven.
